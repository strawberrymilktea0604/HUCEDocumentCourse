Lou has not only managed a photography studio in Boston, Massachusetts for many years, he also specializes in advertising, corporate, commercial, editorial & fine art. Lou has traveled extensively to 60 countries, 48 US states & has covered 13 summer/winter Olympic Games. Lou’s work has been exhibited all over the world in institutions like the Smithsonian, DeCordova Museum, Kayafas Gallery, Roxbury Community College, Emerson College, Cape Cod Museum of Art, Bridge Gallery, etc. with works collected by Harvard University, Wellesley College & University of Texas. Having served on the Boards of Directors of American Society of Media Photographers, Griffin Museum of Photography, Photographic Resource Center & others, Lou also helped found the school Center for Digital Imaging Arts. Throughout Lou’s career, he has published at least a dozen books including Final Exposure: Portraits from Death Row, Speedlights + Speedlites, travel+PHOTOGRAPHY, Saint Petersburg, Russia & contributed to myriad more. Currently, Lou is focused on documenting the 54 countries of Africa in www.panAFRICAproject.org
